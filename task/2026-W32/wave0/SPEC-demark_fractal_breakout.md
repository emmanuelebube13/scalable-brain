# SPEC-demark_fractal_breakout
**Source:** row 48 of forex_swing_strategies.csv · https://www.forexfactory.com/thread/37521-simple-pure-mechanical-h4-system-ea-needed
**Conviction (author's):** EXPERIMENTAL

## 1. Hypothesis
When price breaks beyond a recently confirmed DeMark LevDP=2 swing point on H4, the break signals that the side which produced the last local extreme has lost control: stops clustered just beyond the fractal are triggered, short-term momentum traders pile in, and the retest-and-failure dynamic around a visible, widely-watched level propels price far enough to outrun the small structural stop behind the last opposite fractal. The edge should persist because LevDP=2 fractals are objective, identical on every trader's chart, and therefore concentrate real stop and entry orders at exactly the levels this system trades; the mandatory confirmation lag means the system buys strength after a pause rather than at the extreme itself, filtering out the noise breaks that eat naive tick-level breakout systems. It degrades in low-volatility mean-reverting regimes where every level break fails, and the 2007-era source offers no performance evidence — hence EXPERIMENTAL.

## 4. Entry — long
All conditions are evaluated on the **decision bar t** = the most recently CLOSED H4 bar. A "red circle" is a swing high occurring at bar k per §3; its **confirmation bar** is c = k+3.

1. **Fresh confirmation event:** a red circle's confirmation bar IS the decision bar, i.e. c = t (equivalently k = t−3). Emission is event-driven: exactly one OrderIntent per newly confirmed red circle, never a standing re-emission (§10 #5).
2. **Level unexceeded (staleness rule, exact):** `max(High[k+1 … t]) < High[k]` — no bar since the fractal occurred, including the confirmation bar itself, has reached the level. If any bar's High touched or exceeded High[k], the level is stale and there is **no emission**. (A level also becomes superseded when a NEWER red circle confirms; since emission happens only on fresh confirmations, the newest valid level is always the one traded — "only the most recent unexceeded red circle is valid; ignore stale levels".)
3. **Stop anchor exists and is sane:** let j be the occurrence bar of the most recent blue circle with confirmation bar j+3 ≤ t, level Low[j]. Require (a) such a circle exists in history and (b) `Low[j] − 3 pips < entry_price` (stop strictly below entry). If either fails, **skip the signal** — no valid stop, no trade (§10 #9).
4. Emit one `OrderIntent`, `direction = 1`, `decision_bar = t`.

- **Entry type:** `buy_stop`
- **Entry level (exact formula):** `entry_price = High[k] + 4.0 pips + 1.0 pip = High[k] + 5.0 pips` (4.0-pip noise buffer + the 1.0-pip cost-model spread convention, converted per pair via `calculate_pips`). Condition 2 guarantees `Close(t) ≤ High(t) < High[k] < entry_price`, so the pending price is never already through the market at decision-bar close (contract validation passes).
- **expires_after_bars:** **3 decision-frame (H4) bars** = 12 H1 simulation bars if the engine counts simulation bars (Wave 2 translation note, §10 #11). **No-overlap arithmetic:** two swing highs can occur at closest 3 bars apart (bars k and k+3 with both intervening highs below both), so consecutive same-side emissions are ≥3 decision bars apart. An order emitted at c is live during c+1…c+3 and is expired at §3.2 step 1 of bar c+4; the earliest next same-side intent (decision bar c+3) is admitted at §3.2 step 6 of bar c+4 — expiry strictly precedes admission, so **same-side pending-pending overlap is impossible even in the tightest case**. Cross-side overlap (buy stop and sell stop live simultaneously) is intrinsic and is recorded, not patched (§10 #8).
- `size_fraction = 1.0`.

## 5. Entry — short
Exact mirror of §4 on the same decision-bar framework. A "blue circle" is a swing low at bar j (Low[j] strictly below the lows of the 2 bars on each side), confirmation bar c = j+3.

1. **Fresh confirmation event:** c = t.
2. **Level unexceeded:** `min(Low[j+1 … t]) > Low[j]` — no bar since occurrence has traded down to the level.
3. **Stop anchor:** most recent confirmed RED circle (occurrence k, k+3 ≤ t, level High[k]) exists and `High[k] + 3 pips > entry_price`; else skip.
4. Emit one `OrderIntent`, `direction = −1`, `decision_bar = t`.

- **Entry type:** `sell_stop`
- **Entry level:** `entry_price = Low[j] − 4.0 pips − 1.0 pip = Low[j] − 5.0 pips`.
- **expires_after_bars:** 3 decision-frame (H4) bars; same no-overlap arithmetic as §4.
- `size_fraction = 1.0`.

Long and short setups are NOT mutually exclusive: a red circle and a blue circle can confirm on the same or nearby bars, and both orders may be pending at once. This is the strategy's documented two-sided design ("stop and reverse when opposite setup triggers"); its lifecycle consequences are in §10 #8.

## 6. Stop
- **Initial stop (long):** `stop.price = Low[j] − 3.0 pips`, where Low[j] is the most recent confirmed blue circle as of decision bar t (§4.3). **(short):** `stop.price = High[k] + 3.0 pips`. Exact formula, fixed at emission, anchored to decision-bar-knowable confirmed levels — never to the fill (the pending fill price is unknowable at emission; realized R ≠ declared R when the fill gaps through the entry level, resolved honestly by F3/F6).
- **Declared risk R** = |entry_price − stop.price| = High[k] − Low[j] + 8.0 pips (long) / High[k] − Low[j] + 8.0 pips (short).
- **move_to_breakeven_on:** none (no breakeven mechanism exists in the source).
- **trail (expressible proxy for the source's swing-to-swing fractal trail):** `trail_atr_multiple = 1.5`, ATR(14) on H4. Ratchet semantics per F9, initialised at the initial stop so it never starts wider: long `trail(b) = max(trail(b−1), Close(b) − 1.5 × ATR14_H4(b))`, short mirrored. Under H1 resolution the ratchet updates at each completed H1 bar close using the most recently COMPLETED H4 ATR value (engine-side, causal by construction). **What this replaces:** the source's "move the stop to each new opposite-side circle as it appears" is a per-bar, structure-anchored stop re-emission, which contract v2 cannot express (stops are declared per-intent; the strategy cannot observe new fractals after emission and amend the order). The ATR ratchet is the nearest expressible analogue: it only tightens, it follows price, and 1.5×ATR(14,H4) approximates the typical distance from price to the most recent opposite-side LevDP=2 fractal on H4 majors. The full cost of this substitution is documented in §10 #6 and §11.

## 8. Filters
- **None exist in the source and none are added:** no trend filter (no higher-timeframe gate — H4 is the only frame), no session/time-of-day gate, no volatility gate (ATR sizes the trail; it is NOT an entry veto), no news/calendar gate (no such data exists and none is requested).
- **Staleness gate** (§4.2/§5.2) is an entry condition, not a filter; evaluated on H4, knowable at close of decision bar t (§9).
- **PROXY FLAG (prominent, per the no-invented-data rule):** the entry formulas embed a **1.0-pip spread** (`+spread` / `−spread` in the source) because no real spread series exists in the data. The 1.0-pip value is the F10 cost-model convention used as an explicit, declared proxy. It is used inside the signal itself (entry trigger level), so it is flagged here and in §10 #4, and it is the only non-price proxy anywhere in this spec. Note the engine additionally charges the F10 spread (1.0 pip) plus slippage (0.5 pip) on the actual fill — total friction therefore exceeds what the 2007 author modeled, which biases results conservatively (§10 #4).

## 11. Expected behaviour
- **Trade frequency:** LevDP=2 fractals are frequent (a red or blue circle every few bars on H4), but the emission gate is strict: fresh confirmation + level untouched for 3+ bars + fill within a 3-H4-bar window at 5 pips beyond the level. Expect roughly **1–5 trades/month/pair** (~15–60/pair/year; ~75–300/year on the 5 live pairs, more once Wave-1 pairs land). Per-fold pooled counts should clear `low_confidence` on multi-pair cells but may be thin on single-pair 6-month OOS cells.
- **Character:** breakout-momentum profile — modest win rate, winners carried by the ATR ratchet for 1–5 days, losers cut at the structural stop (−1R declared, occasionally worse through weekend gaps per F6 since pendings can fill at a gapped Monday open). Because entries require a 5-pip break beyond the level within 12 hours of confirmation, fills cluster in genuinely moving markets; quiet ranges produce mostly expired orders.
- **What would fail the gates:** (1) **fidelity loss in management** — the swing-to-swing fractal trail is this strategy's core, and the 1.5×ATR ratchet is a stand-in whose behaviour diverges most in exactly the trending moves the strategy lives on (§10 #6); a fail here does not refute the author's EA, and a pass is evidence about the variant, not the original — the report must say so; (2) the 3-bar expiry truncates the live EA's persistent orders, systematically reducing fill rate (§10 #5); (3) double spread friction (1.0 pip in the trigger + F10 on the fill) penalises a strategy whose edge per trade is small (§10 #4); (4) dual-position whipsaw fills (§10 #8) inflate loss frequency in chop; (5) F5 stop-before-target pessimism at H1 resolution against a tight structural stop.
- **Is the author's EXPERIMENTAL conviction justified by the rules as written?** Yes — if anything it is the honest ceiling. The system is genuinely 100% mechanical (objective fractal detection, fixed buffers, no discretion anywhere), which is rare and valuable in this CSV; but it is a 2007 forum thread with **no documented performance**, explicit parameter sensitivity (LevDP, buffer pips, confirmation lag), and two of its three exit mechanisms are inexpressible in contract v2 — what gets backtested is a degraded variant whose management core has been replaced by a proxy. EXPERIMENTAL correctly encodes "clean mechanics, unverified edge, faithful-impossible exits"; a gate pass under this conservative spec would be a genuinely interesting signal precisely because the deck is stacked against it.
