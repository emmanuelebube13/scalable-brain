**5.1 — The `GBP_USD` row is mislabelled.** `GBP_USD` is GBP-**based**; its quote currency is
USD. On a USD account quote == account, the rate is 1, and the correct size is **50,000** —
the exact number my table flags as the broken one. 39,370 requires GBP as the *quote*
currency, i.e. an instrument like `EUR_GBP` on a USD account (50,000 / 1.27). The arithmetic
was right, the instrument name was not. **The defect class is real for genuinely GBP-quoted
crosses; it was never real for `GBP_USD`.**

**5.2 — My §11 restates §3's numbers under a CAD account heading, and the two are

currency **is** the account currency — nothing to convert, and 50,000 is arithmetically
correct. My table calls that "conversion missing". Following it means "fixing" correct code,
on the one module where a wrong fix moves real money.

Your read on what survives is also right: the USD_JPY rule of thumb holds (JPY ≠ CAD, the

queue clock is the same instinct — a number that is technically available but means nothing
is worse than no number.

NaN rows instead of refusing" is closed by removal, not by fix, and it must be re-opened
