## O-30 landed: `check_cell_degeneracy` re-scoped to degeneracy residual to declared geometry — the dry-run now passes the guard

**Found while:** implementing the O-30 owner decision of 2026-09-17 (re-scope, option 2 of the
three in `audit/reports/gatekeeper_feature_basis.md` §2.5).
**Where:** `src/gatekeeper/train.py` (new `GEOMETRY_CV_FLOOR = 0.10`, new `strategy_geometry_cv`,

**What:** the guard inferred identity-keying from bimodal per-cell approval — sound while

at call time (the same list the manifest publishes as `features`), so identity-keyed constancy
cannot masquerade as geometry-explained if the basis ever reverts.
