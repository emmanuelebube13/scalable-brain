killed the producer at startup. The guard now fires only when the artifact's own
`feature_names_in_` contains `strategy_id` (conservative raise kept when `feature_names_in_` is
unavailable), and the `UNKNOWN_STRATEGY_ID` gate applies only when `known_strategies` is
non-empty.
