## 2. The HMM had zero walk-forward folds, and the speed was the only symptom

**Status: FIXED.**

The retrain reported `acc=1.000 kappa=1.000` at D1, H4 and H1, and `regime_causal` coverage
jumped from ~39% to 100% labelled / 0 UNKNOWN. The log said why, quietly:

```
[D1] no walk-forward folds (series < 36mo) -> filtered-only fallback
```

Two causes compounding:

* `hmm_regime.load_features` hardcoded `"timestamp" >= '2021-08-20'` — ~5 years of history.
  **This is the exact hazard Work Order 01 named as a thing to check for.** It was checked
  for in `build_structural.py` and never checked here.
* `generate_folds` now clamps `series_end` to `HOLDOUT_CUT_DATE` (2023-01-01).

Usable span 2021-09-28 → 2023-01-01 = **15.1 months** against a 36-month minimum train, so
`default_folds` returned `[]` at every granularity.

Fixed with `FEATURE_ANCHOR_DATE = "2005-01-01"`. Folds 0 → **28** per granularity.

**The owner caught this from the runtime**, not the output: the broken run finished in ~80
seconds where a healthy one takes ~35 minutes. One fit instead of 28, on a quarter of the
data. Worth remembering — an unexplained speed-up is a symptom.
